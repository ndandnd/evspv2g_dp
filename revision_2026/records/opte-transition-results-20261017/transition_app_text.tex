Table~\ref{tab:transition} repeats the comparison of Section~\ref{ssec:method} with six random task samples per cell for 20--35 locations.
With 20 and 25 locations the direct MIP finds an optimal or near-optimal schedule within 1{,}800 seconds on 53 of 54 inputs.
As in Table~\ref{tab:grid}, the gap of the MIP over the CG routes grows with the number of tasks per location.
Eight inputs were also run in the grid study; at 30 locations two of them changed outcome between the two runs (starting schedule only in one run, gaps of 0.28\% and 0.00\% in the other), while the costs of the MIP over the CG routes were identical.
