In these runs the outcome of the direct MIP depends mainly on the map.
With 15 locations it proves optimality at every task count within 1{,}190 seconds.
With 30 locations its root LP takes 927--1{,}810 seconds; from 240 tasks it often still has only its starting schedule at 1{,}800 seconds, although it proves optimality by 2{,}511 seconds.
From 45 locations it has only its starting schedule at 1{,}800 seconds for every task count.
The gap of the MIP over the CG routes grows with the number of tasks per location: for a fixed map it grows with the number of tasks, and for a fixed number of tasks it shrinks as the map grows, from 25.9\% with 15 locations to 2.6\% with 120 locations at 240 tasks.
CG time grows with both, from under a minute with 15 locations and 120 tasks to 2.2--2.6 hours with 120 locations and 960 tasks.
