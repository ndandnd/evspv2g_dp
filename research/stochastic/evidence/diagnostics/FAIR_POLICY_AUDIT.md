# Fair-policy formulation and certificate audit

Audited `fair_policy_v9/run.py`, the wave-7 protocol, imported model assembly and dispatch setup, and the 30 completed MIP JSONs in `snapshots/fair_policy_validation/campaigns/weather_fair_policy_wave7`. No worker or campaign files changed; no campaign jobs launched.

**Conclusion:** no substantive mathematical error found in the matched finite-pool expected-cost tie-break. The important limits are the conditional certificate scope, finite routes, full-day training recourse, solver tolerance, and the three unfinished SAA optimality proofs.

- **Objective and shared commitments are correct.** The common vector is integer route multiplicities `x`, integer BESS count `N`, and continuous common initial state `s0`. Each scenario has separate continuous generation, charge, discharge and subsequent states; each terminates at the same `s0`. Trip coverage is included once. Fixed route/asset costs enter once; scenario operating costs receive equal weights. The minimax epigraph bounds every scenario's operating cost, and its objective adds fixed costs. Appending a zero coefficient to the expected-model objective therefore gives the correct secondary objective on the same variables.
- **Certificate scope:** stage 2 solves minimum sampled expected cost subject to `fixed + epigraph <= U1 + 1e-5`. Its bound is a lower bound only for that constrained finite-pool problem. The final worst-cost suboptimality bound is at most `U1 - L1 + 1e-5`, plus numerical feasibility/integrality effects; it is not an exact lexicographic proof. All nine observed stage-1 gaps are zero, and their primary objectives match the corresponding pure minimax results to numerical precision. Thus the 450/900-second allocation does not leave unresolved primary search quality in these results. Secondary optimality still means the reported solver gap/tolerances.
- **Matching verified:** within each arm all ten policies have one identical pool hash: solar+BESS 313 columns, V2G/noBESS 1,919, V2G+BESS 419. All three methods at a given seed use identical sampled dates. Although `weather()` regenerates indices from the seed rather than reading `parent_indices`, all 27 generated sample lists equal those recorded. Matching applies within arms; the three arms use different pools. The outer release/config hashes also validate against the snapshot.
- **Rounded deployment checks:** dispatch rounds integer commitments; native MIP checks use unrounded values. Policies 23, 26 and 29 had integer errors up to `1.9935e-6`. Independent fixed-policy LPs and per-scenario dispatch LPs establish zero training shortage after rounding, and worst costs equal their pure minimax incumbents within `1.82e-12`, approximately `1e-5` below the caps. True rounded mean costs exceed the saved secondary objectives by `0.000061873`, `0.000090520`, and `0.000105811`. Use replayed values when describing deployed policies. Full records are in `fair_rounded_tie_checks.json`.
- **Budget and remaining gaps:** native solver runtime totals 2,872.20994 seconds (0.79784 hours). Cases 11, 14 and 17 (V2G/noBESS SAA) stop at 900 seconds with gaps 1.4454%, 1.7220% and 2.4080%. Their actual runtimes reach 900.03512 seconds: 900 seconds is the requested solver limit, not a strict observed ceiling. Report solver runtime separately from allocated resource ceilings and measured scheduler CPU usage.

Required interpretation and final validation:

1. Never compare the top-level `objective` or `pool_bound` across minimax and tie cases without labeling the objective: tie fields contain conditional **mean** cost; pure minimax fields contain **worst** cost. `primary_objective_of_final` can contain slack epigraph/recourse and need not equal a fixed policy's true minimum worst recourse. Recompute training mean and maximum from per-day optimized recourse.
2. Validate all native solution vectors and rounded commitments, source/identity/hash chains, complete days, physical constraints, cyclic states, and daily oracle lower-cost/shortage ordering. Parent is independently performing these checks. A fixed-policy training LP is recourse evaluation, not refitting.
3. The route set is frozen and no source objective-specific full-route bound transfers. These are finite-pool policy comparisons, not complete-route integer optimality certificates. The protocol correctly leaves `full_lp_lower_bound` null.
4. Tie-break training optimizes full-day perfect-information recourse. Its expected-cost guarantee is not a causal-dispatch cost guarantee. Causal evaluation assumes the current slot's average solar is observed. Any performance statement applies to 2022 development, these fixed route pools, three sampled seeds, and the specified frozen controller. Comparing policy-specific feasible-day means alone can change the set of days; report complete failure/shortage results and paired/common-feasible cost support.
5. Reduced causal mean penalty at essentially unchanged optimal training worst cost is evidence consistent with primary-objective degeneracy; it does not establish a universal absence of robust conservatism. Do not treat a worse time-limited SAA incumbent as a certified disadvantage of the SAA optimum.
