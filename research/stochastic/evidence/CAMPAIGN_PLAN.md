# EVSP–V2G execution plan — 10 September 2026

Decision: start with stochastic optimization, not RL. The tractable first extension commits complete truck profiles, integer stationary-storage count and common initial storage state before weather is observed; continuous storage and fossil dispatch adapt after the entire day's weather is revealed. This is an explicit two-stage foresight benchmark. It is not a causal intraday controller or adaptive routing algorithm.

Read [the independent literature review](REVIEW.md) and shared evidence (original research artifact `SHARED_EVIDENCE.md`; not included in this export). SAA, robust charging and RL charging already have close precedents. A plausible paper needs a specific operational finding about truck commitment versus stationary storage under uncertain renewables; merely applying SAA is insufficient. The earlier six-trip finite-pool pilot did not establish meaningful savings under unlimited fossil supply.

## Wave 1 — executing

160 cases: 20/60 trips × solar scale 1/2 × charge-only, charge-only+BESS, V2G-only, V2G+BESS × (2023 mean + SAA 8/24/64 with seeds 11/29/47). Travel times, traction energy and electricity/fossil prices are deterministic. No DR calls are present. All cases share mandatory trip coverage. Nested samples use training 2023 only. 2022 evaluation is independent of fitting but was used in the submitted manuscript, so it is development evidence, not a fresh scientific holdout.

Exact source DP is called on the sum of raw scenario balance/capacity duals. Probabilities are already in the master objective and must not be applied again to the returned duals. No randomized approximate pricing is used. The numerical certificate is scoped to the discretized common-profile LP and tolerance 1e-6; its lower bound is RMP objective minus number of trips times tolerance. Artificial columns must be zero for certification. Positive artificial mass is unresolved, never a full-family infeasibility proof.

Each terminal CG pool passes an independent fixed-profile time/location/SoC reachability check before it is frozen and hashed. Scaglione MIPs use only that saved pool, preserve native logs and return pool-only bounds/gaps. MIP residuals, coverage and integrality are checked separately. A certified CG LP does not make the finite-pool MIP globally optimal over all integer truck routes.

First results are diagnostic. Once all successful CG pools are collected, create their validated union within each identical physical configuration (points, PV, arm), freeze it as a NEW pool with full contributing provenance, and solve each policy on that common pool in a NEW campaign. This removes differential pool availability as an explanation of comparative policy performance. Do not rewrite wave-1 identities or results. Bound limitations still apply.

Report daily cost distributions and paired mean savings, 14-day moving-block bootstrap intervals (at least 1,000 replicates), all three seed results, sample-size sensitivity, runtime, CPU allocation, solver status, bound/gap and memory. Flag that bootstrap intervals are conditional on the chosen policy and one observed weather year. Never pool scenario-seed replicates as independent observed years. Unlimited generation makes zero weather infeasibility largely structural.

## Wave 2 — next after validation gates

Select operational configurations using development data and record the selection. Introduce clearly specified fossil/generation scarcity and compare expected-cost SAA with a finite-scenario minimax or budgeted robust model and validated representative-day reduction. Before capped experiments, implement and validate true Phase I with energy and coverage artificials; do not repurpose economic big-M artificials as infeasibility certificates. Evaluate all methods at equal computational budgets and common service requirements. Reduction must be evaluated against unreduced samples with matched random seeds, retained extreme days and independent evaluation. Preserve raw daily trajectories and training-only scaling. Obtain a fresh weather period/site before final scientific testing; freeze model selection first.

Falsification gates: stop a claimed advantage if it disappears in common-pool comparisons, across seeds, under equal budgets, or out of sample. If savings remain negligible under unlimited generation, quantify that finding and focus effort on scarcity/commitment rather than expanding an uninformative factorial indefinitely.

## Wave 3 — sequential operation

Build a rolling-horizon LP/MPC baseline using only information available at dispatch time and shared decisions until forecast histories diverge. Compare full-day foresight against causal dispatch to quantify the information advantage. Then study adaptive charging with committed service duties. Existing columns fix charging profiles; adaptive charging needs a changed column representation and a validated pricing or decomposition formulation. Do not label scenario-dependent fixed-route costs as adaptive routes.

## RL gate

RL is a later challenger to the validated causal LP/MPC controller, not the first paper's core. Proceed only if observed nonlinearities, discrete real-time actions or online runtime create a measurable weakness in the optimization baseline. Specify state, actions, safety/feasibility enforcement, forecast observations, seed variation and training budget, and evaluate on exactly the same unseen weather and trip-service requirement. No publication timeline is guaranteed.
